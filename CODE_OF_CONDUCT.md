# Code of Conduct

YukkitMC wants a community where players, developers and first-time contributors can take part without harassment. This applies to the Yukkit Launcher just as it does to every other YukkitMC project.

## What we expect

Everyone taking part in issues, discussions and pull requests should:

- Be respectful, even when you disagree strongly.
- Criticize ideas, code and behavior, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and give useful, actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy, personal boundaries and intellectual property.
- Keep reports factual and discussions on topic.
- Follow GitHub's Terms of Service.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, threats, discrimination or targeted insults.
- Hate speech or degrading comments about personal characteristics.
- Doxxing, sharing private information, or posting private messages without permission.
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
- Spam, impersonation, malicious submissions or deliberate disruption.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports misconduct in good faith.

## Scope

This covers YukkitMC's GitHub spaces, and any conduct presented as official participation in the project.

## Reporting

For anything on GitHub, use GitHub's **Report content** option. For other moderation concerns, contact a YukkitMC maintainer privately through a channel the organization makes available. Please don't put sensitive personal details in public issues.

Security problems have their own process: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Moderation

Maintainers may ask for edits; hide or remove content; close discussions; limit participation; or report serious violations to GitHub. What happens depends on context, severity, repetition and the safety of the people affected.

Reports are handled discreetly where possible. Confidentiality can't be guaranteed when the law or platform policy requires disclosure.

## Disagree well

Disagreement is a normal part of building software. Harassment is not. Challenge designs, improve the code, and help each other learn.
